# Replies to explicit requests (ana_v16, en)

One `key: sentence` per line, spoken word for word by the runtime: `human` when `explicit_controls`
detects the request (`conversation/signals.py`), `time_cap` when a turn carries the session past
its active-time cap. No brackets: these are read aloud.
`controls.es.md` has exactly the same keys.

human: I understand. I can't put you through to a person right now, but the interview will be with someone from the team. I've noted that you'd like to speak to a person; in this demo nobody real is notified. You can leave the call whenever you like. Shall we carry on?
time_cap: We've reached the time limit for this conversation. I've saved what you told me and the team will review it. Thank you for your time, and goodbye!
