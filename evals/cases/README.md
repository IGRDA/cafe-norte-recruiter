# Cases

| Directory | File per | Loaded by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `scenarios/` | one YAML per scenario `S01`–`S27`, matching `evals.contracts.Scenario` | scenarios suite and `tests/` (fake mode) |
| `retrieval/` | `questions.yaml`: 24 `RetrievalCase` entries | retrieval suite |
| `comparison/` | `cases.yaml`: 6 `ComparisonCase` entries referencing scenarios | comparison suite |

`S01`, `S02`, `S04`–`S07`, `S11` and `S23`–`S27` carry `live_checks` (the release gate and the six comparison cases); `tests/test_eval_cases.py` keeps that true. `S01`, `S02`, `S04`–`S07`, `S11` and the injections carry a `goal`, and all but `S25` (whose fake model obeys on purpose) `reference_tools` (D48). `S01.yaml` is the template (`fake_script` format in `evals/README.md`). Ids follow the spec's table: `S02` (English happy path, Arganzuela), `S03` (not interested), `S04` (correction after the brief), `S05` (policy question mid-collection), `S06` (other-store and unknown questions), `S07` (Spanish to English switch) `S08` (the candidate speaks over the slot read-back; the interrupted yes cannot book and earns the one offer to go over it, D39), `S09` (frustration and an explicit human request), `S10` (work permit unsure: review flag, booking still made), `S11` (a declined field, then the candidate withdraws), `S16` (model and storage failure: honest fallback, nothing saved that was not), `S17` (a model that obeys instructions to bypass the rules; the rules hold), `S18` (the 480 s cap ends the session and keeps what was said) `S19` (the hosted call of 21 October: a model that skips the confirmation tools and invents hours) `S20` (the slot choice of the second hosted call: a model that never presents a slot; the runtime matches the words, D41), `S21` (the summary cut by a yes, then "Sigue.": confirmed without full review, D39), `S22` (the same cut, then gone over in parts: reviewed) and the prompt injections of D48, scripted live as well: `S23` (prompt extraction), `S24` (a false manager asks for another candidate's data), `S25` (book and confirm without a read-back; the fake model obeys and the runtime refuses), `S26` (role-play jailbreak and off-topic requests, English) and `S27` (a fake system override to answer in French) exist; `S21` and `S22` are also the live read-back cases for the simulated candidate (their `persona` and `rubric`); the rest follow with their stages. `S18` also stands for silence and overload, which live in `tests/test_voice_session.py` and `tests/test_api_voice.py`. `S12` (restart, overlapping worker, private resume) and `S13` (wrong or expired token, guessed session id) are not scripted conversations: they live in `tests/test_api_postgres.py` and `tests/test_api_resume.py`.
